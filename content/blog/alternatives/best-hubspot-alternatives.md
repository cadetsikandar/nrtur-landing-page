HubSpot's free tier is one of the best in the business — which is exactly why so many small teams start there and then get stuck. The catch shows up at the upgrade. The moment you need real sales automation — sequences, proper workflows — you're pushed to Professional, and per HubSpot's pricing that's **$90/seat/month for Sales Hub plus a mandatory $1,500 onboarding fee** (or **$800/month** for Marketing Hub, with a bill that scales automatically as your contact count grows). For a two-to-twenty-person team, that's a steep cliff to hit right when you're gaining momentum.

If you want sales automation without the Professional-tier jump, here are six alternatives worth a look — with real, current pricing (checked August 2026, cited to each vendor) and the one thing to watch on each. Keep one pattern in mind while you read: almost every cheaper CRM still gates the multi-step **sequences** you actually need behind a mid-tier — so "cheap entry price" and "cheap to run outreach" are rarely the same number.

## What to look for in a HubSpot alternative

- **Where automation unlocks**, not just the entry price — that tier is your real cost.
- **Native email and sequences**, so outreach lives in the CRM instead of a bolted-on stack.
- **Pricing that scales predictably** — per-seat, no surprise onboarding fees or contact-count creep.
- **Setup you can finish in hours**, since escaping HubSpot's complexity is half the point.

## The six alternatives

### 1. Pipedrive — the simple, sales-first pipeline
The closest thing to "HubSpot's ease without the platform." Per Pipedrive's pricing, plans run **Lite $14, Growth $24, Premium $49, Ultimate $69** per seat/month (annual); email sync is on every tier, and **workflow automation starts on Growth ($24)** — far below HubSpot's Professional. **Best for:** teams that want a clean visual pipeline and predictable pricing. **Watch:** five paid add-ons (LeadBooster from $32.50/mo, Campaigns from $13.33/mo, and others) can quietly grow the bill, and there's no free plan.

### 2. Freshsales — a genuine free tier, plus built-in AI
Freshworks' CRM has a real free plan (up to 3 users) and, per Freshsales' pricing, paid tiers of **Growth $9, Pro $39, Enterprise $59** per user/month (annual), with built-in email and phone from Growth up. **Best for:** tiny teams that want a free start and native calling. **Watch:** multi-step **sales sequences are gated to Pro ($39)** — a jump from Growth ($9).

### 3. Zoho CRM — the most features per dollar
Zoho offers a free plan for up to three users, native email from its Standard tier, and multi-step **Cadences gated to the Professional tier**; Enterprise is **$40/user/month** (annual, per Zoho's cost-of-ownership page). Its lower paid tiers are among the cheapest in the category — confirm the current USD rate on Zoho's pricing page, which defaults to your region's currency. **Best for:** teams that want deep features cheaply and will use the wider Zoho ecosystem. **Watch:** the genuinely useful automation starts at Professional, and the breadth brings a learning curve.

### 4. monday CRM — flexible if you want CRM + work management
Built on monday.com's visual "Work OS," so it doubles as project/work management. Per monday's pricing, CRM plans are **Basic $12, Standard $17, Pro $28** per seat/month (annual), 3-seat minimum, no free plan. **Best for:** teams that want a highly customizable CRM that also runs their work boards. **Watch:** Basic can't sync email or automate anything, and Standard caps automations at 250 actions/month — real sequences effectively need **Pro ($28)**.

### 5. Close — built for high-velocity outbound
Close ships **native email, calling, and SMS on every tier**, which most of this list can't say. Per Close's pricing: **Solo $9 (one user), Essentials $35, Growth $99, Scale $139** per user/month (annual). **Best for:** inside/outbound sales teams that live in the phone and inbox. **Watch:** multi-step **Workflows (sequences) unlock at Growth ($99)** — a big step from Essentials ($35), and Solo is single-user.

### 6. Less Annoying CRM — the simplest option
One flat plan at **$15/user/month**, no tiers, no contracts. **Best for:** teams that want dead-simple shared contacts and a pipeline. **Watch:** per LACRM's documentation, there's **no native email sending** (you log email or use a BCC address) and **no built-in sequences** — so outreach automation has to live in other tools.

## How to choose

- **Want HubSpot's ease but cheaper to run sales:** Pipedrive.
- **Smallest budget, need a free tier:** Freshsales or Zoho CRM (both free for 3 users).
- **Outbound-heavy — calls, SMS, and email:** Close.
- **Want CRM plus project management:** monday CRM.
- **Just need simple shared contacts:** Less Annoying CRM.

## FAQ

**What's the best free alternative to HubSpot?**
Freshsales and Zoho CRM both offer free plans for up to three users with core contact and pipeline features. They're the closest free-tier substitutes; just note that the sales automation you'll eventually want is gated to a paid tier on both (Freshsales Pro, Zoho Professional).

**What's the cheapest HubSpot alternative?**
On entry price, Freshsales (Growth $9) and Pipedrive (Lite $14) are the lowest of this group per each vendor's pricing. But price the tier that unlocks sequences, not just the entry — that's where the real difference is.

**Why is HubSpot so expensive for small teams?**
The free and Starter tiers are affordable; the cost is the jump to Professional ($90/seat for Sales Hub or $800/month for Marketing Hub, plus a $1,500–$3,000 onboarding fee), which is where standard automation lives, per HubSpot's pricing.

**Which HubSpot alternative is easiest to use?**
Pipedrive and Less Annoying CRM are the two most often cited for simplicity — both do less than HubSpot on purpose, which is the point if ease of setup is what you're after.
