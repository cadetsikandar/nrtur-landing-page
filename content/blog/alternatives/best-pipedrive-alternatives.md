## What's actually pushing you off Pipedrive?

- **Automation limits** → you want higher (or uncapped) headroom.
- **Add-on creep** (LeadBooster, Campaigns, Projects, Smart Docs, Web Visitors — each $13–41/mo) → you want more included in the base price.
- **No free plan** → you want a genuine free tier to start.
- **Need marketing too** → you want an all-in-one, not a sales-only pipeline.

## The six alternatives

### 1. HubSpot — the all-in-one with a real free tier
If your reason for leaving is "I want more than a pipeline," HubSpot is the natural step up: sales, marketing, and service in one platform with a strong free tier. The trade-off is the upgrade — per HubSpot's pricing, real sales automation lives on **Sales Hub Professional at $90/seat/month plus a $1,500 onboarding fee** (Marketing Hub Professional is $800/month). **Best for:** teams that want marketing + sales together and will use the free tier first. **Watch:** the Starter-to-Professional cliff is HubSpot's version of Pipedrive's caps.

### 2. Freshsales — a free tier and built-in phone
Freshworks' CRM gives you what Pipedrive doesn't: a real free plan (up to 3 users) and native calling. Per Freshsales' pricing: **Growth $9, Pro $39, Enterprise $59** per user/month (annual). **Best for:** small teams that want a free start with built-in email + phone and Freddy AI. **Watch:** multi-step **sequences are gated to Pro ($39)**.

### 3. Close — more outbound firepower, no add-ons
Where Pipedrive charges extra for outreach tooling, Close builds it in: **native email, calling, and SMS on every tier**. Per Close's pricing: **Solo $9 (one user), Essentials $35, Growth $99, Scale $139** per user/month (annual). **Best for:** high-velocity outbound teams that want calling and cadences native. **Watch:** multi-step **Workflows unlock at Growth ($99)**, a notable step up, and Solo is single-user.

### 4. Zoho CRM — cheaper breadth
If you're leaving on price, Zoho packs more features into lower tiers, with a free plan for up to three users, native email from Standard, and multi-step **Cadences at the Professional tier**; Enterprise is **$40/user/month** (annual, per Zoho's cost page). Confirm the current USD rate on Zoho's pricing page (it defaults to regional currency). **Best for:** teams that want maximum capability per dollar and the wider Zoho ecosystem. **Watch:** breadth means a steeper learning curve than Pipedrive's focused UI.

### 5. monday CRM — a flexible work-OS alternative
If you found Pipedrive too rigid, monday's board-based CRM is the opposite — highly customizable, and it doubles as project/work management. Per monday's pricing: **Basic $12, Standard $17, Pro $28** per seat/month (annual), 3-seat minimum. **Best for:** teams that want to bend the CRM to their process and run work boards in the same tool. **Watch:** email sync and automation start at Standard, and automations are capped by monthly action limits until **Pro ($28)**.

### 6. Less Annoying CRM — if Pipedrive still felt like too much
Some teams leave Pipedrive not for more, but for less. LACRM is one flat plan at **$15/user/month**, radically simple. **Best for:** teams that want shared contacts and a basic pipeline with zero configuration. **Watch:** per LACRM's documentation, there's **no native email sending and no sequences** — it's a system of record, not an outreach engine.

## How to choose

- **Want more than a pipeline (marketing too):** HubSpot.
- **Want a free tier + built-in calling:** Freshsales.
- **Outbound-heavy, want calling/SMS/sequences native:** Close.
- **Leaving on price, want more features:** Zoho CRM.
- **Found Pipedrive too rigid:** monday CRM.
- **Found Pipedrive too complex:** Less Annoying CRM.

## FAQ

**Is there a free alternative to Pipedrive?**
Yes — Pipedrive has no free plan, but HubSpot, Freshsales, and Zoho CRM all offer free tiers (Freshsales and Zoho for up to three users). They're the go-to options if a genuine free start is what pushed you to look.

**What's a better alternative to Pipedrive for automation?**
It depends on scale. Close and HubSpot offer more powerful multi-step workflows than Pipedrive's capped automations, though both gate that power behind a higher tier (Close Growth $99, HubSpot Professional $90/seat). If you want automation included without a mid-tier jump, that's the gap the sales-first newcomers target.

**Why do people leave Pipedrive?**
The most common reasons are the automation caps (50/150/250 by tier), the paid add-ons that raise the real cost, the lack of a free plan, and — for teams that need marketing — its sales-only focus.

**What's the cheapest Pipedrive alternative?**
On entry price, Freshsales (Growth $9) and Zoho CRM (free tier) are the lowest of this group per each vendor's pricing. As always, check the tier that unlocks sequences, since that's usually the number that matters for a sales team.
