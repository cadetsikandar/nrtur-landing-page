Salesforce is the most powerful CRM on the market — and for a small team, often more than they can use or afford. The reasons teams go looking for an alternative are consistent: the cost climb, the **annual-only contracts above the entry tier**, the setup complexity that usually needs an admin or a partner, and Premier support billed at an extra **30% of your license**. If you genuinely need Salesforce-grade depth, nothing here fully replaces it — but most small sales teams don't, and these six give you real sales capability without the enterprise overhead. Every price is from each vendor's own pricing page, checked August 2026.

## What to look for in a Salesforce alternative

- **Sales capability without the admin burden** — power you can run yourself.
- **Predictable pricing** — no annual lock-in or paid-support surprises.
- **Setup in days**, not a partner engagement.
- **Only the depth you'll actually use** — the rest is cost and complexity you carry for nothing.

## The six alternatives

### 1. HubSpot — the approachable all-in-one
The most common step "down" from Salesforce for teams that still want breadth: a free tier plus sales and marketing in one platform. The cost is the Professional jump — Sales Hub Professional at $90/seat/month plus a $1,500 onboarding fee, per HubSpot's pricing. **Best for:** teams that want platform breadth with far easier setup. **Watch:** HubSpot's Pro cliff is its own version of Salesforce's climb.

### 2. Zoho CRM — the closest "cheaper Salesforce"
Zoho matches more of Salesforce's breadth than anything else here, for a fraction of the price: a free plan (up to 3 users), native email from its Standard tier, multi-step Cadences at Professional, Enterprise at **$40/user/month** (annual, per Zoho's cost-of-ownership page), plus the wider Zoho ecosystem. **Best for:** teams that want Salesforce-like breadth on a budget. **Watch:** the breadth brings a learning curve, and confirm Zoho's lower USD tiers on its pricing page (it defaults to regional currency).

### 3. Pipedrive — if you only need the pipeline
If Salesforce felt like a jumbo jet to cross the street, Pipedrive is the opposite — a focused visual pipeline. Per Pipedrive's pricing: **Lite $14, Growth $24, Premium $49, Ultimate $69** per seat/month (annual), email on all tiers, automation from Growth. **Best for:** sales-first teams that want simplicity and predictable per-seat pricing. **Watch:** automation is capped by tier, and outreach add-ons cost extra.

### 4. Freshsales — enterprise-style features at SMB pricing
Freshworks' CRM gives you a free tier (3 users) and built-in phone + AI, with paid tiers **Growth $9, Pro $39, Enterprise $59** per user/month (annual). **Best for:** small teams that want a polished, AI-assisted CRM cheaply. **Watch:** sales sequences are gated to Pro ($39).

### 5. Close — for outbound-heavy teams
Where Salesforce charges to extend into calling and texting, Close builds them in: **native calling, SMS, and email on every tier**. Per Close's pricing: **Solo $9 (one user), Essentials $35, Growth $99, Scale $139** per user/month (annual). **Best for:** high-velocity inside sales. **Watch:** multi-step sequences unlock at Growth ($99).

### 6. monday CRM — flexible and visual
A board-based, highly customizable CRM that doubles as work management — a lighter answer to Salesforce's configurability, without the admin. Per monday's pricing: **Basic $12, Standard $17, Pro $28** per seat/month (annual), 3-seat minimum. **Best for:** teams that want to shape the CRM to their process themselves. **Watch:** automations are capped by monthly action limits until Pro.

## How to choose

- **Want breadth, cheaper and easier:** HubSpot or Zoho CRM.
- **Just need a sales pipeline:** Pipedrive.
- **Outbound-heavy — calls, SMS, sequences:** Close.
- **Smallest budget / free tier:** Freshsales or Zoho CRM.
- **Want to customize without an admin:** monday CRM.

## FAQ

**What's the best alternative to Salesforce for a small business?**
It depends on whether you want breadth (HubSpot or Zoho CRM come closest) or simplicity (Pipedrive). Use the criteria above — sales capability without the admin burden, predictable pricing, fast setup, and only the depth you'll actually use — and match them to your workflow.

**What is the cheapest Salesforce alternative?**
On entry price, Freshsales (Growth $9) and Zoho CRM (free tier) are the lowest of this group per each vendor's pricing; Pipedrive (Lite $14) if you want a pure pipeline. As always, price the tier that unlocks sequences, not just the entry.

**Is HubSpot a good Salesforce alternative?**
Yes for teams that want platform breadth with easier setup and a free tier — it's the most common like-for-like step down. The trade-off is HubSpot's own Professional-tier cost once you need standard automation.

**Why do small businesses leave Salesforce?**
The common reasons are the cost climb ($25 Starter to $175 Enterprise per user), annual-only contracts above Starter, setup complexity that usually needs an admin or partner, and paid Premier support (+30% of license). For teams that don't need enterprise depth, it's simply more than the job requires.
